Meeting at the Santa Clara County Shooting Range, Blue Monday Skeet is a league dedicated to the fun skeet shooting. There are 4 leagues per year running 10 weeks in length. Teams are made up of 5 shooters, and members compete at both a team and individual level.  Awards are given for both team and individual success.


Blue Monday Skeet meets every Monday and we welcome new members.
